A Montana kitchen, moved to the Gulf

Coastal Cowboy Social is one man’s two homes on one plate: the Western steak-and-game country he cooked in for years, and the Florida coast he moved to six years ago and decided to stay on.

The cook

Douglas Starz grew up cooking in Pennsylvania — a short drive from the Maryland line and the Chesapeake Bay, which is where his seafood ran was learned. He later owned The Dugout, an on-mountain barbecue restaurant at 9,000 feet in Big Sky, Montana. Wild game, steaks and smoke are what that altitude teaches you.

“We want to bring a little bit of Montana or the West down to Florida and combine it with the Florida seafood as well.”

The town he chose

He’s lived in the Ruskin area for about six years now — long enough to pick the building and the customer base on purpose. “I love the area,” he says. Palmetto, Sun City, Bradenton, Riverview and Brandon are all about a 20-minute drive from the front door, which is exactly the point of putting a place like this here.

The building

102 W Shell Point Road is a 1925 building with good bones, right where Ruskin meets the bay. It’s being renovated into three rooms — a Florida-style dining room, a Western-themed bar, and an outdoor pavilion — finished with barnwood shipped down from Montana. The upstairs apartment becomes a members-only speakeasy in phase two, planned for 2027.
