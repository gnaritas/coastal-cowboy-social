The ranch side

Game first. Steaks always.

“We want to bring a little bit of Montana or the West down to Florida and combine it with the Florida seafood as well” — the owner, Douglas Starz, on why elk and bison share a board with Gulf catch.

The board

  • Elk & bison the opening headliners — wild game is the lead of this menu, not a novelty line
  • Bison, ranch-to-plate for the opening a whole bison comes from a friend’s ranch — USDA-inspected, then driven to Florida
  • Venison, to follow the vendor hunt is on; when it’s set, it goes on
  • Three or four steak cuts this place is steak-focused at its core
  • Barbecue items several of them, carried down from the owner’s mountaintop barbecue days

Why this kitchen knows game

Before Ruskin, Douglas Starz owned The Dugout — a barbecue restaurant on the mountain at 9,000 feet in Big Sky, Montana. That operation is where the wild-game, steak and smoke program was built, cut by cut, season after season. Now it’s being replanted on the Gulf.
