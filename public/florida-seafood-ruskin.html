The coast side

Bay cooking, cowboy hands

Half of this concept was learned on the Chesapeake. The other half is being written here — on Shell Point Road, where Ruskin meets the bay.

Where the seafood fluency came from

Douglas Starz grew up cooking in Pennsylvania, a short drive from the Maryland line and the Chesapeake Bay. Crab pots, oyster shanties, whatever came off the water that morning — that is the kitchen education underneath the seafood half of this menu.

Where it lands

  • Florida seafood the Gulf side of the board, from the waters the shop sits on
  • Served with the grill seafood here meets a steak-and-game kitchen — scallops next to elk, the way the West eats it
  • Lunch and dinner the seafood board runs both services from day one
