What you send us

Messages from this site’s form go straight to the shop’s inbox — the name, email and note you type, nothing more. We use them to answer you and to send the opening-day announcement to people who asked for it. We do not sell, rent or trade your details.

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. Pages load from our own host with our own stylesheet; there is no third-party script on any page.
