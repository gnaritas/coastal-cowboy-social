Three rooms, one address

Dinner, drinks and a band — outside

The building does three settings on purpose: a Florida-style dining room for the table, a Western bar for the nightcap, and a pavilion for the weekend.

The bar

The bar is the loudest room in the building — and by design. The interior is being finished with barnwood brought down from Montana, and the mounts are going up as fast as the framers finish them. This is the cow-town half of the concept: cocktails, cold beer, and a room that looks like it drove down from the Big Sky.

The pavilion

  • Live bands, weekends live music and other entertainment are planned for pavilion nights from the opening plan forward
  • Cocktail seating outside the pavilion is built for drink-in-hand service under the open Florida sky
  • Outdoor service food and drinks, served out — no reservation needed to stand in a good band’s crowd

And later: the upstairs

Above the dining room is a two-bedroom apartment today. It’s planned to become a private, membership-based speakeasy — phase two, expected in 2027. When the details are set, they go on this site first.
